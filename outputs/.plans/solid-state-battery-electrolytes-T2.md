# Researcher Brief T2 — Performance Benchmarks & Recent Breakthroughs

**Project:** Deep research on solid-state battery electrolytes.

## Scope

Collect current performance benchmarks and notable research breakthroughs (roughly 2022–2026, with key foundational older work where needed). Compare solid-state against liquid-electrolyte lithium-ion baselines. Do NOT cover company commercialization (T3's track).

## Questions to answer

1. Benchmark conductivity numbers: best-reported room-temperature ionic conductivities for sulfides (e.g., LGPS ~10 mS/cm class), oxides, halides, polymers; typical liquid-electrolyte conductivity (LiPF6 in carbonate, ~10 mS/cm) for comparison. Attribute every number to a source.
2. Cell-level metrics from recent papers: areal capacity, critical current density (CCD) for Li metal, cycle life (e.g., >1000 cycles), energy density claims (Wh/L, Wh/kg) — distinguish lab-level from claimed commercial.
3. Notable breakthroughs 2023–2026: record-setting electrolytes (e.g., chloride/halide superionic conductors, high-entropy or doped sulfide/oxide systems), interface engineering results, thin solid electrolyte membranes, anode-free designs.
4. How solid-state cell energy-density claims compare with best commercial Li-ion cells (roughly 250–300 Wh/kg class as of mid-2020s) — cite independent assessments (DOE, Argonne, BNEF, academic reviews) not just company press.
5. Solid-state with Li metal vs. with graphite/silicon anodes: expected advantage and who claims what.
