Renogy sells three 12V battery lines that RV owners compare most: the Pro Series Smart Lithium (self-heating, Bluetooth), the Core Series (budget LiFePO4), and the Deep Cycle AGM. The Pro is the only one of the three that can charge below freezing. It charges down to -4°F because its heater warms the cells first.

This page documents the 100Ah model in each line, sourced from renogy.com product pages and FAQs. Specs were verified November 6, 2026.

### How the Pro's self-heating actually works

Renogy's FAQ spells out the conditions. The heater draws power from your charger, not from the battery. It switches on when the cells are between -4°F and 41°F. It needs two things to run: charger voltage at least 0.5V above battery voltage, and more than 4A of charge current per battery. The heater shuts off once the cells pass 50°F, and normal charging takes over.

That has a practical consequence. A weak solar trickle on a cloudy winter morning may not clear the 4A threshold. In that case the heater will not start, and the battery will not charge. Size your solar or DC-DC charger so each battery sees more than 4A when you need winter charging.

The Pro is also parallel-only. If you plan a 24V or 48V bank, the Core line supports series wiring and the Pro does not.

Renogy also sells a Core Mini with "DuoHeat" heating in some regions. That variant is listed with a charge range down to -22°F. Check availability on the US store before planning around it.

AGM numbers are not directly comparable to LiFePO4 numbers. Renogy rates the AGM at 50% depth of discharge, so each cycle delivers about 50Ah of usable capacity. A LiFePO4 cycle at 80% DoD delivers about 80Ah. Per usable amp-hour delivered, the AGM costs roughly 11 times more than the Core Mini.

## Consistency Note: Renogy vs Battle Born in the Cold

Older Renogy lithium batteries without heaters stop charging at 32°F. The current Pro Series charges to -4°F once its heater warms the cells. Battle Born's standard batteries block charging below 25°F, and its Heated models add internal heating. Our [Battle Born vs Renogy comparison](/posts/battleborn-vs-renogy/) uses these same figures.
