## FAQ

### Can you charge a battery with a jump starter?

No, not in the way a charger does. A jump starter delivers a short, high-current burst to crank the engine. A charger feeds a low, steady current over hours to refill the battery. Automoblog draws that line clearly: a charger "provides a lower current to steadily replenish your battery's power," and a jump starter "provides a strong current to reset a dead battery." Some units do both. Craftsman sells a fully automatic charger and jump starter, and Battery Tender's Charge N Start 4120 pairs a 4 amp charger with a 1200 amp jump pack. If you want one device to do both jobs, buy a combo, not a plain jump pack.

### What is the best battery charger jump starter?

There is no single best one, but the Schumacher SC1309 is the model Automoblog names "Best Battery Charger and Jump Starter Combo," rated 4.5. For a wall-powered unit, Battery Tender's Charge N Start 4120 combines a 4 amp charger with a 1200 amp lithium jump pack, enough for gas engines up to 6.0L and diesel up to 4.0L. My pick depends on your garage. If the vehicle sits for weeks, I would take the Charge N Start because it stays plugged in and maintains the battery. If you just want a portable box in the trunk, a standalone jump pack is fine.

### Will a jump starter start a completely dead battery?

Usually yes, if the battery is discharged rather than failed. A jump pack sends a strong current to crank the engine, which is exactly what a dead battery can't do on its own. Battery Tender says its 1200 amp pack "starts full-size vehicles with gas engines up to 6.0L and diesel engines up to 4.0L without jumper cables or a second vehicle." Two limits matter. First, a battery with an internal short or a dead cell won't recover, no matter the amps. Second, very cold batteries resist.

### Can you use a battery charger to jump start?

You can jump-start a car with a battery charger only if the charger has a dedicated engine-start mode. A standard charger can't supply the burst of current a starter motor needs, so connecting one and turning the key does nothing. Craftsman's CMXCESM253 has a 100 cranking amp engine start and a 30a boost mode, which is the feature to look for. The Schumacher SC1280 is a charger only, putting out 13.0 V at 15.0 A, and Automoblog doesn't list it as a jump starter. If your charger lacks an engine-start rating, use a jump pack instead. I wouldn't gamble on a boost mode alone for a large engine.

### What are good brands for a battery jump starter?

The brands that show up repeatedly in the research are NOCO, Schumacher, Battery Tender, Craftsman, CTEK and TOPDON. Automoblog rates the NOCO Genius line across several price tiers, from the budget Genius 1 to the premium Genius PRO50, and lists the Schumacher SC1309 as its top charger and jump starter combo. Battery Tender's Charge N Start line includes the 1100 with a 1000 amp jump pack and the 4120 with 1200 amps. Craftsman covers wheeled garage units, including a 175 cranking amp model with a 25a boost mode. For a truck, check the amps against your engine before buying.
