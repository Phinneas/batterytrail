Battle Born builds its deep-cycle LiFePO4 lineup around two capacities RV owners compare most: the **100Ah 12V** (BB10012) and the **270Ah 12V GC3** (BBGC3). Battle Born also sells a 100Ah 12V GC2 model in a golf-cart (6V-style) case. This page is a single reference for every spec dimension that matters when you're sizing a bank, sourced directly from battlebornbatteries.com. Heated and Smart variants exist for both capacities. They're noted where they differ.

"GC2" and "GC3" are Battle Born case formats. The GC2 is a separate 100Ah 12V battery built in a golf-cart-style footprint. The GC3 *is* the 270Ah battery. Heated variants (BB10012H, BBGC3H) add internal heating for sub-freezing charging. Smart variants add Bluetooth monitoring through Battle Born's Dragonfly IntelLigence system.

## Notes on the Numbers

**Cost per cycle is linear across capacities.** The GC3's ~$0.57 per cycle looks higher than the 100Ah's ~$0.20. But the GC3 holds 2.7 times the energy. Per 100Ah of capacity, the GC3 works out to about $0.21 per cycle. Battle Born prices close to linearly per amp-hour. The GC3 buys capacity density, not a per-cycle discount.

**Cycle life vs. depth of discharge.** Battle Born rates both lines at 3,000 to 5,000 cycles and lists 100% usable depth of discharge. Its product pages do not state the DoD basis of the cycle rating. Like all LiFePO4, shallower cycling extends service life. Cost-per-cycle figures on this page use the 4,000-cycle midpoint.

**Warranty.** Battle Born lists a 10-year limited warranty on both lines. That is longer than the 5-year and 7-year terms common on competing brands. Confirm claim requirements on battlebornbatteries.com before purchase.

**Cold weather.** Both batteries discharge down to -4°F. Neither the standard 100Ah nor the standard GC3 accepts charge when the cells drop below 25°F. The BMS pauses charging and resumes once the battery warms. For cold-bay charging, buy the Heated variant (BB10012H or BBGC3H), which warms the cells before accepting charge.

**Certifications.** Battle Born states its batteries carry listings from Nationally Recognized Testing Laboratories to UL 2054 and UL/CSA 62133-2, plus UL 1642 cell certification. Earlier versions of this page listed UL 1973 for the 100Ah. Battle Born's current safety materials name UL 2054 and 62133-2 instead, so we updated the tables to match.
